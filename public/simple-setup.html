Group separate heats (optional)

Usually not needed: a championship or scholarship is one heat whose name lists its dances, e.g. “Smooth Championship (W, T, F)”, and is detected automatically. Use this only to group separate heats, one per dance. The overall winner is the couple with the most 1st-place marks from all judges across the dances, then most 2nds, 3rds, and so on. See it in Admin → Results.
